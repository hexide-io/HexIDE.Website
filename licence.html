Licence

MIT, both halves, no strings.

The IDE and the language server are both MIT licensed. Use it, ship it in something commercial, or build your own IDE on it: the licence text is the whole of the deal.

The aim is that you can rebrand HexIDE with a configuration file, without forking it. That isn't built yet, and until it is, MIT means you can fork it.

Privacy and security

What leaves your machine.

The core IDE doesn't phone home, collect telemetry, or send your projects anywhere. It reads and writes local files, and its one optional network check, for revoked add-ins, is off until you give it an address.

The bundled AI Chat add-in is the exception, and it should be obvious rather than buried. When you send it a message, it sends that and the whole active file, form layout and captions included, to Anthropic's API, or to another endpoint you configure, with your key. It sends nothing until you do. It takes the key from its settings, or from an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, OPENAI_API_KEY or HEXIDE_AI_API_KEY environment variable, so a key already in your environment is enough.

Nothing here is code-signed or notarised yet, the Windows installer included, and the download section says so for each platform.

Please report a vulnerability privately rather than in a public issue: open a draft security advisory.
